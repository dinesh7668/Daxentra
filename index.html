About the Studio

Digital experiences that work for your business

Your website is often the first impression a potential customer has of your business. A thoughtfully designed digital experience communicates professionalism, builds trust, and makes it easy for visitors to take the next step—whether that’s making a purchase, booking a service, or reaching out to learn more.

At Daxentra, we partner with businesses to create websites and digital products that look great, work seamlessly, and serve a clear purpose. We focus on clean design, reliable technology, and a process that keeps you informed and involved at every step.

How We Work

A clear path from idea to launch

Every project follows a structured process designed to keep things on track, transparent, and collaborative from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start by understanding your business, your audience, and your goals. Through conversation and research, we define what your project needs to succeed.

  2. 02

    Plan & Design

    We map out the structure and create visual designs you can review. You’ll see how your site will look and function before any code is written.

  3. 03

    Build & Refine

    We develop your site with clean, reliable code and test it thoroughly. You’ll have opportunities to review and provide feedback at every stage.

  4. 04

    Launch & Support

    We handle the technical details of going live and make sure everything is working perfectly. After launch, we’re here for ongoing support and updates.

FAQ

Common questions

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with small and growing businesses across a range of industries. Whether you’re a local service provider, a retail brand, a professional firm, or a startup, we tailor our approach to fit your specific needs and goals.

How much does a project cost?

Project costs depend on scope, complexity, and the specific services involved. We provide a clear, detailed estimate after our initial conversation so you know exactly what to expect. There are no hidden fees.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary based on the project’s size and requirements. During our planning phase, we’ll establish a realistic timeline with key milestones so you always know what’s coming next.

Can you work with my existing brand or website?

Absolutely. We can redesign or rebuild your current site while respecting your existing brand identity. If you have brand guidelines, a style guide, or existing assets, we’ll incorporate them into the new design.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing website care and maintenance to keep your site secure, updated, and performing well. We’re also available for future improvements and new features as your business grows.

What do I need to get started?

Just reach out and tell us a bit about your business and what you’re looking for. We’ll take it from there. If you have existing brand materials, content, or a current website, those are helpful but not required to begin the conversation.
