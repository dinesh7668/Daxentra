Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Some quick answers to help you decide whether to apply.

Who can apply for an internship?

Students, recent graduates, and beginners interested in building their IT or professional skills are welcome to apply. Requirements may vary by program.

Do I need prior experience?

Not always. Some tracks may suit beginners. Tell us about your current experience and goals in the application.

How long is the internship?

Duration depends on the program and current schedule. Our team can confirm details when following up on your application.

Are internships paid, and is a certificate provided?

Stipend, fees, and certificate eligibility depend on the specific opportunity and its completion requirements. Please confirm the terms with our team before accepting an offer.

What happens after I apply?

Our team will review your details and contact you about the relevant program and next steps.
